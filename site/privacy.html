Privacy policy

Effective 18 October 2026. Applies to the Open Kiosk Android app.

In short

Open Kiosk does not collect, transmit, share or sell any personal data to its developer or to anyone else. It has no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no accounts.

What stays on your device

  • Your settings: the dashboard address, screensaver, brightness and similar preferences.
  • The settings PIN and the REST API token, stored only as one-way hashes.
  • The MQTT password, if you set one, encrypted with a key held in the Android Keystore.

This data is excluded from Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer, and is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

The dashboard you open

Open Kiosk displays a web page that you choose, such as your Home Assistant dashboard, loaded from your own server. That page may store cookies and local storage on the device, as any browser does. What the page does with data is governed by that site, not by Open Kiosk.

Optional features that send data

These are off until you turn them on in the PIN-protected settings, and they only talk to destinations you configure:

  • MQTT: sends the device's state and diagnostics to your MQTT broker. Diagnostics are the app, Android and WebView versions, device model, free memory and storage, network type, battery level, uptime and the address of the page shown.
  • REST API: answers requests from your own network with the same information, and only to callers holding the token you generated.
  • Text-to-speech: uses the speech engine installed on your device. That engine's own privacy policy applies.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state: to load your dashboard and reload it when the network returns.
  • Run at startup: to bring the dashboard back after a reboot.

Open Kiosk does not request access to the camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos or files.

Children

Open Kiosk is a tool for setting up wall displays and is not directed at children.

Changes and contact

Changes to this policy are published on this page and in the source repository. Questions: info@shoshins.com.
